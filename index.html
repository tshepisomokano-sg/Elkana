Our services

Everything a household needs

From the everyday wash to the once-a-year wedding dress, Elkana handles fabric, leather and upholstery under one roof.

Wash, dry & iron

Everyday laundry, folded or pressed and ready to wear. Priced per kilogram.

Dry cleaning

Upholstery
